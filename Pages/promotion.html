Official promotion

Popularity is evidence, not authority.

Repeated independent adoption is a strong signal, but official support also requires generality, deterministic semantics, authority preservation, compatibility, verification, and maintainability.

Promotion boundary

Review can be triggered by evidence. Promotion requires governance.

Usage, votes, and community interest can inform review, but they do not automatically create official support.

Evaluation criteria

Signals considered before official adoption

Promotion evaluates whether a community pattern can become a stable, supportable PTSIP capability without weakening project authority.

  • Repeated need across independent projects
  • Generalizable semantics rather than one repository convention
  • Preserves project-owned authority and owner decisions
  • Can be represented and verified deterministically
  • Can be supported through repository contracts and release lifecycle

Decision path

Evidence and authority remain separate

1. Community signal

Adoption, repeated use, and discussion can indicate that a problem deserves formal review.

2. Conformance review

The candidate must still satisfy compatibility, deterministic behavior, and supportability requirements.

3. Governance decision

Only an explicit governance decision can establish official PTSIP support.
