FAQ

Common questions

Answers to common questions about Community Modes, authority boundaries, source declarations, and official support.

Authority boundary

FAQ explains the model; it does not replace authoritative repository contracts.

Normative requirements remain in the designated PTSIP Specification, ADRs, registries, schemas, and other authoritative repository records.

Is a Community Mode an official PTSIP feature?

No. It becomes official only after deliberate adoption through authoritative repository and release boundaries.

Can a Community Mode be hard-coded for one environment?

Yes. Environment-specific assumptions are acceptable when they are explicit. Promotion later evaluates what can be generalized safely.

Is Community Mode the same as explicit, template, or hybrid?

No. Community Mode is an incubation and distribution concept. Canonical PTSIP source declaration modes are defined by the authoritative repository.

Does PTSIP translate Community Primitive content?

No. Community Primitive content is displayed in the original language submitted by its author. Readers may use browser or external translation tools if needed.

Does popularity automatically promote a Community item?

No. Popularity and adoption are evidence that may justify review. Official support still requires conformance review and an explicit governance decision.
