Authority boundary

The website explains policy. It does not define policy.

Normative requirements remain in the designated PTSIP Specification, ADRs, registries, schemas, and other authoritative repository records.

Community lifecycle

Experiment freely. Promote deliberately.

1

Project Custom Policy

A project solves a real environment-specific need, including hard-coded assumptions when they are explicit.

2

COMMUNITY

A reusable pattern is published with compatibility, limitations, provenance, and real usage evidence.

3

PROMOTION_CANDIDATE

Maintainers accept the problem for formal evaluation. This is not yet official support.

4

OFFICIAL

Support is established through authoritative repository contracts, implementation, verification, and release.
