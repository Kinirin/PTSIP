8. STANDING COMMERCIAL AUTHORIZATION AND COMMERCIAL COOPERATION DUTY

8.1 Standing Authorization

PTSIP provides Standing Commercial Authorization. Separate case-by-case approval, a signature from the Licensor, and a monetary license fee are not required for Commercial Use that satisfies this Section.

Standing Commercial Authorization applies when the Commercial User:
(a) expressly accepts the applicable version of this License;
(b) expressly accepts the Commercial Cooperation Duty as a material and essential condition of Standing Commercial Authorization;
(c) identifies the PTSIP Version or Revision used;
(d) completes Commercial Use Registration within the Commercial Adoption Window; and
(e) remains in compliance with the applicable attribution and cooperation duties.

The Licensor's silence or failure to respond to a valid registration does not prevent Standing Commercial Authorization from taking effect.

8.2 Commercial Adoption Window

A Commercial User may begin evaluating, integrating, or adopting PTSIP for a Commercial Purpose before registration.

The Commercial User must submit a valid Commercial Use Registration no later than ninety calendar days after the first intentional Commercial Use.

If registration is completed within that period, Standing Commercial Authorization applies from the first day of that Commercial Use.

The ninety-day period may not be repeatedly restarted, reset, or manipulated to avoid registration or cooperation duties.

If registration is not completed by the deadline, Ordinary Noncompliance begins and is governed by the cure and suspension rules in Section 11. Standing Commercial Authorization remains conditionally available during the applicable cure period for a user acting in good faith to complete registration.

8.3 Commercial Use Registration

Registration must identify:
(a) the individual or legal entity using PTSIP;
(b) the product, service, system, business activity, or internal commercial function for which PTSIP is used;
(c) the applicable PTSIP License version;
(d) the PTSIP Version or Revision used, with at least one precise identifier required and both version and immutable revision included when reasonably available;
(e) the date Commercial Use began;
(f) a responsible cooperation contact or accountable project contact;
(g) the primary Official Cooperation Channel record for that registration; and
(h) an affirmative statement expressly accepting both the applicable PTSIP License and the Commercial Cooperation Duty.

Registration does not require disclosure of revenue, profit, customer lists, unrelated proprietary code, or confidential financial information.

8.4 Annual Commercial Cooperation Report

For every continuous twelve-month period of Commercial Use, the Commercial User must submit at least one Commercial Cooperation Report no later than ninety days after the end of that period.

The report must provide substantive, truthful information reasonably obtained through actual use, including:
(a) PTSIP versions or revisions used;
(b) usage scope and approximate duration;
(c) material coding-agent, tool, CI, platform, or integration environments;
(d) compatibility findings and workarounds;
(e) known policy ambiguities or architecture-governance findings;
(f) known false positives, false negatives, incorrect classifications, failures, or policy bypasses;
(g) meaningful improvement suggestions, missing capabilities, or usability observations;
(h) a description of modifications made directly to PTSIP and the status of their upstream submission; and
(i) reasonably shareable aggregated operational observations useful to PTSIP improvement.

A Commercial User may truthfully report that no known defect or improvement was found. The user is not required to invent defects or contributions. However, a statement such as "no feedback" does not by itself satisfy the report when usage, version, environment, integration, and reporting-period information can reasonably be provided.

8.5 Material Findings

A Material Finding discovered during Commercial Use must be shared no later than ninety days after a responsible participant has sufficient information to reasonably believe that the finding exists. A security-sensitive finding or a finding reasonably expected to have immediate and broad material impact should receive an initial notice within thirty days, with reasonably available supporting detail provided no later than the ordinary ninety-day deadline. Reporting serves shared improvement; it does not by itself create responsibility under this License for how another user independently applies PTSIP.

8.6 Commercial PTSIP Modifications

If a Commercial User modifies PTSIP itself and uses that modification in Commercial Use, the PTSIP-specific modification must be offered back to the PTSIP project in source form, as a patch, pull request, equivalent implementation, or sufficiently complete technical contribution.

The submission is due no later than the earlier of:
(a) ninety days after the modified PTSIP implementation enters Commercial Use; or
(b) the due date of the next Commercial Cooperation Report.

This obligation applies to the PTSIP modification. It does not require disclosure of unrelated proprietary product source code.

8.7 Ending Commercial Use

Commercial Use does not end merely because:
(a) revenue is zero;
(b) the activity or company is unprofitable;
(c) expenses exceed revenue;
(d) the PTSIP-attributable financial benefit cannot be measured;
(e) a product is pre-revenue, beta, subsidized, investment-funded, or temporarily free; or
(f) PTSIP is one of many technologies used.

Commercial Use ends only when PTSIP and applicable PTSIP-Derived Material are no longer materially used to develop, operate, maintain, support, improve, distribute, or otherwise enable the relevant Commercial Activity.

8.8 Final Commercial Cooperation Report

When Commercial Use ends, the Commercial User must submit a Final Commercial Cooperation Report within ninety days if material Commercial Use occurred after the period covered by the most recent Commercial Cooperation Report.

Ending Commercial Use does not cancel duties that accrued before Commercial Use ended.

8.9 Resumption

If Commercial Use resumes, the Commercial Cooperation Duty resumes automatically.

The Commercial User must update or renew the applicable registration within ninety days after resumed Commercial Use.

A materially different category of Commercial Use requires an updated or additional registration.

8.10 No Profit-Based Avoidance

For avoidance of doubt, no Commercial User may avoid this Section merely by asserting that the user earned no revenue, operated at a loss, could not isolate PTSIP's financial contribution, had not yet reached profitability, received only indirect economic value, or temporarily provided the relevant activity without charge.

The duty follows Commercial Use, not profitability.

11. COMPLIANCE, CURE, SUSPENSION, AND REINSTATEMENT

11.1 Ordinary Noncompliance

Failure to submit a required registration, report, finding, attribution correction, or PTSIP modification by its deadline is Ordinary Noncompliance.

A ninety-day cure period begins on the missed deadline.

The compliance periods in this License are intended to encourage voluntary cooperation and reasonable correction, not to create traps for users acting in good faith.

Standing Commercial Authorization remains conditionally available during that cure period for a user acting in good faith to cure the omission.

If the violation remains uncured when the cure period expires, the affected Commercial Authorization is automatically suspended.

11.2 Effect of Suspension

During suspension, the user may perform only activities reasonably necessary to:
(a) cure the violation;
(b) cease unauthorized PTSIP use;
(c) protect security or data integrity; or
(d) comply with applicable law.

Accrued obligations remain due.

11.3 Automatic Reinstatement

Authorization suspended solely for Ordinary Noncompliance is automatically reinstated when all overdue obligations causing the suspension are fully cured.

Reinstatement does not erase the historical violation and does not reset reporting anniversaries.

11.4 Material or Intentional Noncompliance

Intentional removal or falsification of attribution, knowingly false cooperation reports, deliberate concealment of a Material Finding to avoid reporting, deliberate concealment of a required PTSIP modification, or repeated manipulation of adoption, suspension, or resumption periods to evade reciprocity duties is Material Noncompliance.

For Material Noncompliance, the Licensor may provide written notice requiring cure within thirty days. If the violation is not cured within that period, the Licensor may suspend the affected authorization.

Repeated or persistent Material Noncompliance may require renewed written authorization after cure.

11.5 Persistent Noncompliance

If a required obligation remains uncured for one hundred eighty days after its original due date, the affected Standing Commercial Authorization may be treated as terminated.

A user that fully cures the outstanding obligations may request or obtain a new Standing Commercial Authorization by completing the then-applicable registration requirements.

11.6 No Monetary Penalty Created by This License

Nothing in this Section limits remedies that applicable law may otherwise provide.
