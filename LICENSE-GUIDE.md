# PTSIP License Guide

This guide explains PTSIP Community Reciprocity License 1.0 in plain language. It is not a substitute for LICENSE. If this guide conflicts with LICENSE, LICENSE controls.

## Core philosophy

> PTSIP does not require contribution as a price for access. PTSIP requires reciprocity so that knowledge gained through PTSIP does not become a one-way private benefit.

PTSIP is publicly available because the project is intended to be inspected, used, tested, improved, and shared. The license is not designed primarily to collect money. It is designed to preserve original authorship and to make organized or commercial users return useful PTSIP-related knowledge and improvements when they obtain them.

This is a source-available community reciprocity license. It is not an OSI-approved Open Source license.

## What counts as Commercial Use?

Commercial Use is based on purpose, not accounting results.

It includes use intended to develop, operate, support, improve, market, sell, or materially enable a business activity. Revenue does not need to exist.

Examples that remain Commercial Use:

- a paid product that has not launched yet;
- a startup product with zero revenue;
- a product or company operating at a loss;
- a free beta intended to become a business;
- an advertising-supported or customer-acquisition service;
- internal use that reduces development cost, context use, reasoning cost, token use, or coding-agent credits;
- consulting or client work;
- an investment-funded commercial development project.

A company does not avoid cooperation duties by saying that it cannot measure how much profit came from PTSIP.

## Research can be long or short

PTSIP does not require a project to predict when research will end.

Institutional Research Use:

- requires attribution;
- requires an annual Research Cooperation Report only after use continues for more than 12 months;
- requires a Research Cooperation Summary when material research ends or is first materially published if the same period has not already been substantially reported;
- requires Material Findings to be shared within the period defined in LICENSE.

If a research project later becomes a commercial development effort, commercial duties begin when the Commercial Purpose begins. Earlier research duties do not disappear.

## What must be shared?

PTSIP asks for PTSIP-related knowledge, not unrelated company secrets.

Useful reciprocity may include:

- compatibility results;
- coding-agent behavior findings;
- policy ambiguities;
- false positives and false negatives;
- failure patterns;
- test or evaluation results;
- reproducible examples;
- anonymized or aggregated usage observations;
- documentation corrections;
- architecture or policy proposals;
- PTSIP patches and modifications.

The license does not require disclosure of unrelated proprietary product code, customer-confidential information, credentials, personal information, or unrelated trade secrets.

## What if nothing went wrong?

You are not required to invent defects or send meaningless patches.

A valid report may truthfully say that no material defect was found. It must still identify the real use context, PTSIP version, relevant environment, integrations, and other required information that is reasonably available.

The purpose is to return actual knowledge gained through use.

## Code reuse and PTSIP architecture

The license protects copyrightable PTSIP expression rather than claiming ownership of every similar software idea.

Protected material includes PTSIP-specific code, Specification text, schemas, policy definitions, rule identifiers, conformance vectors, documentation, and copyrightable selection or arrangement.

Examples likely to remain PTSIP-derived when PTSIP material was used as the source include:

- porting PTSIP code from Python to another language;
- translating PTSIP normative text into a machine contract;
- copying PTSIP schemas and renaming fields;
- using PTSIP source or Specification as input to an AI system and distributing the transformed implementation;
- reproducing a substantial distinctive arrangement of PTSIP-specific protected material.

A separately created implementation is not automatically PTSIP-derived merely because it independently reaches a similar general architecture or method.

There is therefore no fragile list of specific module paths that must be updated whenever PTSIP is refactored.

## Standing Commercial Authorization

Commercial users do not need to wait for individual approval.

A commercial user receives Standing Commercial Authorization by:

1. accepting the current PTSIP license;
2. registering within 30 days of first Commercial Use; and
3. continuing to satisfy attribution and cooperation duties.

There is no default monetary license fee.

See COMMERCIAL-USE.md for the current registration and reporting procedure.

## Missed reports and correction

The license is designed to prefer correction over punishment.

Ordinary missed obligations have a cure period. If the user cures the omission, ordinary suspended authorization can be automatically reinstated.

Intentional attribution falsification, deliberately false reporting, concealment, or repeated attempts to evade reciprocity are treated more seriously.

## AI use

PTSIP may be used for training, fine-tuning, evaluation, dataset construction, agent development, or compatibility testing.

PTSIP attribution should appear in a reasonably discoverable model card, dataset record, training record, system documentation, technical documentation, research record, or equivalent place. The license does not require every model output to repeat the notice.

Commercial AI use follows commercial reciprocity. Institutional noncommercial AI research follows research reciprocity.

## Historical license versions

The license attached to a particular PTSIP revision governs the permissions granted with that copy, subject to applicable law.

Later PTSIP license versions do not silently rewrite earlier copies. A later PTSIP release may use a later license and may require acceptance of that later license for that later release.
